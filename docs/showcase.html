01A match, step by step

(?P<w>\w+)\s+\1 over go go no

05What lowers onto the core engine

Pattern::lower

10 patterns

A pattern the core engine can run is handed to it as a @string.Regex. The rest stay here — that is the line between the two engines.

lowers → @string.Regex

  • abc
  • a(b+)c
  • [a-z]+
  • (?<y>\d{4})

stays here

  • \bab\b
  • ^ab$
  • (a)\1
  • a(?=b)
  • \X
  • (?>ab)

\bab\b and ^ab$ are the two that surprise people: the core engine's word boundary is ASCII, and its $ does not sit before a trailing newline, so neither is the same assertion this library means.
